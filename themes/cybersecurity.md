# Cybersecurity

How AI agents change security spending, and which layers get paid.

## Research

- [Binance heist: what it means for our cyber basket](https://theme-radar.github.io/research/binance-heist-cyber-basket.md) (2026-11-04): The largest crypto theft on record ran through a poisoned package, a fake job offer, an over-privileged token and an internal AI tool. No change to the basket, but it points to identity and software supply chain. A fear-driven slowdown in AI agents helps the basket. A return-driven one is the real risk.
- [Cybersecurity hardware: Fortinet and F5](https://theme-radar.github.io/research/cyber-hardware-ftnt-ffiv.md) (2026-11-02): Both names fell about 20% on earnings in the same week. The moat is being physically in the data path inside the customer's building, which AI cannot vibe-code. Own the category at equal weight, but wait: the entry conditions and kill triggers are set out name by name.
- [Passport Control for Robots: SAIL vs OKTA](https://theme-radar.github.io/research/passport-control-for-robots.md) (2026-10-09): AI agents pay the identity registry, not the login. Governance is priced per identity, login per employee. Within listed identity, SailPoint owns the better-placed layer at the lower multiple.
