## Research

- [Natera: Is the Sudden CEO Change a Buying Opportunity?](https://theme-radar.github.io/research/natera-ceo-transition.md) (2026-11-11): Buy in two parts, starting at about $312. Natera fell 28% in eight trading days: about half in a market sell-off, the rest after the CEO resigned without a stated reason. Sales grew 34% and cancer test volumes hit a record. At 14.6x sales the price is roughly fair on our numbers (probability-weighted value $315), so the second half waits for $275 or the 26-Feb-27 results.
- [Channel check: what a Nobel Prize-winning mRNA lab at Penn told us about AI, monkeys and the wet lab](https://theme-radar.github.io/research/penn-mrna-channel-check.md) (2026-11-04): A Penn mRNA scientist confirmed that monkeys, wet-lab capacity and labour are the scarce inputs in drug discovery, and flagged two risks: AI still needs heavy human checking, and China is now the cheapest source of lab kit.
- [If frontier AI labs are becoming the next generation of biotech platforms, how should we position?](https://theme-radar.github.io/research/ai-frontier-labs-biotech.md) (2026-10-31): Frontier AI labs are buying biotechs, building wet labs and starting their own drug programmes. Design gets cheap and validation stays scarce, so the physical layer gets paid: molecule makers, lab tools, testing services and data owners.
